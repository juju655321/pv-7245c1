Jupiter Rockets is the home base for Josh Ott: guitarist, builder of online stores, and maker of songs, stories, and games. It's where the music, the creative projects, and the ecommerce work all lift off from the same pad.

About me

Meet the pilot

I'm Josh Ott, and I work in all things ecommerce while surviving in a world of AI. I live in Fairmont, West Virginia.

For more than 15 years I've built and run online sales channels, from starting Goodwill's Amazon and eBay stores to managing direct-to-consumer stores and marketplaces at Liquidity Services, with catalogs of 50,000 to 100,000 SKUs on Shopify, BigCommerce, Amazon, eBay, Walmart Marketplace, and TikTok Shop. Before that, I spent nearly a decade managing a Borders bookstore.

Music came first, though. I played guitar in King Belvedere, a riff-driven Baltimore hard-rock band that shared stages with Cinderella, KIX, L.A. Guns and Bang Tango. Jupiter Rockets is where that creative side gets its own launch pad again, alongside the stores, songs, stories, and games I build today.

Today I'm also an independent e-commerce consultant, helping brands and sellers grow on Shopify, Amazon, eBay and TikTok Shop.

  • 2021–2026 · E-Commerce Manager, DTC, Liquidity Services: ran stores and marketplaces across Shopify, BigCommerce, eBay, Amazon, TikTok Shop, Walmart Marketplace and more.
  • 2013–2021 · E-Commerce Specialist, Liquidity Services: listings, promotional pricing, and weekly sales and inventory reporting.
  • 2011–2013 · E-Commerce Specialist, Eastern Performance Cycles: Magento, eBay, and Amazon inventory, HTML, and SEO.
  • 2009–2011 · Assistant E-Commerce Manager, Goodwill Industries: helped launch Amazon, eBay, Barnes & Noble, and Half.com channels.
  • 2001–2010 · Retail Store Manager, Borders Bookstore.
